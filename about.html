about the journey

Engineer by trade. Wanderer by heart.

Travel is never the destination for me; it is the way a place feels the moment I arrive.

These notes are small and honest — more about a quiet street, an unexpected meal, and the light that made a city feel familiar.

I'm Iniyan, a master's student in electronics and computer engineering based in Dublin. I balance lab work with weekend departures, chasing trains, ferries, and flights to somewhere new.

I've moved through India, Ireland, Sri Lanka, the UAE, Austria, Poland, Hungary, Czechia, and Slovakia on tight budgets that forced me to travel more creatively.

This site is a quieter corner for travel notes, not polished guides. Just honest observations from elsewhere.
