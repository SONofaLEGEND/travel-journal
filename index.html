a little about the wanderer

ENGINEER
BY TRADE.
WANDERER
BY WEEKEND.

I build things that talk over wireless signals for a living — right now, that means finishing a master's in electronics and computer engineering in Dublin. Off the clock, I'm usually somewhere with worse signal and better light: chasing budget routes across borders, taking notes on the small, specific things that don't make it into guidebooks. This is where those notes live — photographs, half-memoir, short bites, no long essays.
